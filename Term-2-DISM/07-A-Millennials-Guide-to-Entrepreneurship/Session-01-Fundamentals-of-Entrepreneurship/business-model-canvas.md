# Business Model Canvas (Template)

Business name: ______________________   Team: ______________________   Date: __________

Tip: write short bullet points. Fill in this order: 1 → 9.

## 1. Customer Segments: who exactly are you serving?
- Example: small clinics in North Nazimabad with no website
-

## 2. Value Proposition: what problem do you solve, and why you?
- Example: a mobile-friendly clinic website with online appointment form, live in 7 days
-

## 3. Channels: how do customers find you and receive the service?
- Example: Facebook page, WhatsApp Business, Fiverr gig, visiting clinics in person
-

## 4. Customer Relationships: how do you keep them?
- Example: free 1-month support, monthly maintenance package
-

## 5. Revenue Streams: how do you earn?
- Example: one-time website fee + monthly hosting/maintenance fee
-

## 6. Key Resources: what must you have?
- Example: laptop, internet, WordPress skills, portfolio, hosting account
-

## 7. Key Activities: what must you do every week?
- Example: build sites, meet clients, post on social media, provide support
-

## 8. Key Partners: who helps you?
- Example: hosting provider, a freelance designer, a content writer
-

## 9. Cost Structure: what will you spend money on?
- Example: hosting, domain names, internet, paid themes/plugins, transport
-

---

## Quick check before you submit
- [ ] Each block has at least 2 bullet points
- [ ] Customer segment is specific (not "everyone")
- [ ] Revenue covers costs (rough numbers written in PKR or USD)
- [ ] At least 5 real people were asked about the value proposition
