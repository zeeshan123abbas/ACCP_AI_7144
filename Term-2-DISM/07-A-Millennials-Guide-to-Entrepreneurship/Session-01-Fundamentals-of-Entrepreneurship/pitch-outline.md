# Pitch Outline (8 slides, 3–5 minutes)

Use one main idea per slide, big text, and one picture or number. Practise with a timer.

| # | Slide | What to say (fill in) | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hook + name** | One sentence that grabs attention. "Every day, ___ people in Karachi struggle to ___." Then your business name. | 20 s |
| 2 | **Problem** | Who has the problem, how painful it is, what they do today. Use one real quote from an interview. | 30 s |
| 3 | **Solution** | What you offer, in plain words. Show a screenshot or demo of your MVP. | 40 s |
| 4 | **Customer & market** | Your exact customer segment and roughly how many of them exist. | 30 s |
| 5 | **Business model** | How you earn: price, packages, repeat income. | 30 s |
| 6 | **Traction** | Proof so far: interviews done, sign-ups, first sale, pilot client. | 30 s |
| 7 | **Team** | Who does what and why you are the right people. | 20 s |
| 8 | **The ask** | What you want: a pilot client, mentorship, funding, feedback. End with contact details. | 20 s |

## 60-second elevator version

> For **[customer]** who **[problem]**, we offer **[solution]** that **[main benefit]**. Unlike **[current option]**, we **[what makes you different]**. So far we have **[traction]**, and we are looking for **[ask]**.

## Checklist
- [ ] No more than 8 slides and about 20 words per slide
- [ ] At least one number (customers, price, time saved)
- [ ] Demo or screenshot included
- [ ] Practised 3 times out loud within time
- [ ] Ready answers for: "Who are your competitors?" and "How will you get the first 10 customers?"
