# A Millennials Guide to Entrepreneurship

![Entrepreneurship](https://img.shields.io/badge/Entrepreneurship-Startup%20Basics-0a7cff) ![Freelancing](https://img.shields.io/badge/Freelancing-Fiverr%20%7C%20Upwork-1dbf73) ![Payoneer](https://img.shields.io/badge/Payoneer-Payments-ff4800) ![Canva](https://img.shields.io/badge/Canva-Design-00c4cc?logo=canva&logoColor=white) ![Trello](https://img.shields.io/badge/Trello-Teamwork-0052cc?logo=trello&logoColor=white) ![LinkedIn](https://img.shields.io/badge/LinkedIn-Personal%20Brand-0a66c2?logo=linkedin&logoColor=white)

> Term 2 (DISM) · 5 sessions · ⏱ ~2 hours each · theory + practical templates

You are learning to build websites with PHP, Laravel and WordPress. This module answers the next question: **how do you turn those skills into income?** You will learn how entrepreneurs think, how teams work, how businesses go digital, how social media grows a brand, and how to start freelancing from Pakistan on platforms like Fiverr and Upwork. Every session ends with a template you can actually use.

## What You Will Learn

- Explain what an entrepreneur is, the types of entrepreneurship and the startup process
- Describe the traits and habits of millennial and Gen-Z founders
- Map a business idea on a **Business Model Canvas** and prepare a short **pitch**
- Explain team roles, norms and player types, and write a **team charter**
- Describe digital transformation, its stages, drivers and technologies
- Use social media platforms to build and market a brand, with a **content calendar**
- Set up a freelancing career: niche, portfolio, profile, proposals, pricing and payments
- Know the basic legal steps for a business in Pakistan (FBR NTN, SECP, PSEB freelancer registration)

## Prerequisites

- None. Basic computer and internet skills are enough.
- A portfolio from Modules 01–06 (websites, PHP apps, WordPress sites) will help a lot in Session 05.

## Module Mini-Project

**My first micro-business plan.** In a team of 2–4, choose one service you can sell with Term 2 skills (for example: WordPress sites for clinics, Laravel dashboards for small shops, landing pages for home bakers). Deliver:

1. A completed Business Model Canvas
2. A team charter with roles
3. A one-page digital plan for the client type you target
4. A 2-week social media content calendar
5. One live freelancer profile (Fiverr gig or Upwork profile) per member, using the checklist
6. A 5-minute pitch (max 8 slides) presented in class
