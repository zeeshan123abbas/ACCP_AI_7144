# Proposal Template (Upwork / email)

Keep it under 150 words. Personalise every proposal; clients can spot copy-paste instantly.

---

Hi [Client name, if shown],

**[Line 1: show you read the job]** You need [their exact requirement, in your words], ideally by [their deadline].

**[Line 2–3: relevant proof]** I recently built [similar project] using [technology]. You can see it here: [live link / portfolio item].

**[Line 4–5: your plan]** My approach:
1. [First step, e.g. confirm pages and features]
2. [Second step, e.g. build and share a staging link]
3. [Third step, e.g. test on mobile and deliver with a short guide]

**[Line 6: a smart question]** Quick question: [one question that shows understanding, e.g. "Do you already have hosting, or should I suggest one?"]

I can start [today / date]. Happy to discuss on a short call.

Best regards,
[Your name]

---

## Checklist before sending
- [ ] First line mentions their project, not you
- [ ] One relevant portfolio link only (the best match)
- [ ] Clear 3-step plan
- [ ] One question at the end
- [ ] Price/timeline matches the job budget
- [ ] Spell-checked
