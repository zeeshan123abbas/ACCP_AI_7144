> Term 1 (CPISM) · 12 sessions × 2 hours · Windows 11, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, OneNote, Outlook, Teams and OneDrive, with AI helpers

## What you will learn

- Use Windows 11 confidently: files, settings, snap layouts, security and shortcuts
- Create professional Word documents with styles, tables, templates, forms and mail merge
- Build Excel workbooks with formulas, functions, charts, data tools and protection
- Summarise data with PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Design PowerPoint presentations with animations, Morph, Zoom, icons and 3D models
- Organise notes in OneNote, mail and calendar in Outlook, and collaborate in Teams and OneDrive
- Use AI writing and analysis helpers (Microsoft Copilot, ChatGPT, QuillBot) responsibly

## Prerequisites

- Basic computer use: mouse, keyboard and typing
- A Windows 10/11 PC (Office also runs on macOS, but screenshots and shortcuts here assume Windows)
- A free Microsoft account for OneDrive, Outlook.com and Teams

## Module mini-project

**"Student Society Starter Kit"** - plan a small event (for example, a coding meetup at your centre) and produce all the office files for it:

1. **Word**: an invitation letter template with your society logo, plus a mail-merged set of letters for 10 guests.
2. **Excel**: a budget workbook (`Income`, `Expenses`, `Summary` sheets) with SUM, IF, XLOOKUP, a chart, a PivotTable of expenses by category, and a protected `Summary` sheet.
3. **PowerPoint**: a 6-8 slide pitch deck using a consistent design, one Morph transition and one Summary Zoom.
4. **OneNote**: a notebook with sections `Planning`, `Meetings`, `To-Do` (use tags).
5. **Outlook & Teams**: a calendar invite for the planning meeting and a Teams channel where all files are shared from OneDrive.

Submit a single OneDrive folder link containing every file. Use an AI helper for at least one draft and write one line in the deck about how you checked its output.
